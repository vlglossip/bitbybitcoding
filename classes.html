Become a Bit by Bit Student

Learn with Purpose. Build with Confidence.

Our program is designed to introduce coding to learners of all ages: from curious upper elementary school students to motivated teens and adults looking to dive into tech. We offer six dynamic class that grow with you.

All of our classes offered are self-paced and available on demand. These lessons are pre-recorded on YouTube and are taught by our experienced tutors.
